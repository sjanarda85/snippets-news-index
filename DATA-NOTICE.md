# Data notice

**Contains data from The GDELT Project, https://www.gdeltproject.org/** (GDELT is open for use and redistribution with citation and a link to the project). Not affiliated with or endorsed by GDELT.

- **Headlines and links** belong to their publishers. They are republished here only as short headlines with a link back to the source, for a pre-live trial. If you are a publisher or a person named in a headline and want an item removed, open an issue in this repository or write to the contact given on the Snippets site, with the page URL. Credible complaints are actioned within 4 hours (see `blocklist.txt`).
- **Place data** (`geo/`): country and state boundaries from [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/) (public domain); city names and coordinates from [GeoNames](https://www.geonames.org/) (CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Details in `geo/ATTRIBUTION.md`.
- **Outlet country list** (`data/DOMAINSBYCOUNTRY-ALLLANGUAGES.TXT`): from the GDELT Project's published domain-by-country list.
- GDELT-derived fields (tone, themes, place tags) are machine-generated and can be wrong. Do not treat a place tag as a statement about where an event happened.
